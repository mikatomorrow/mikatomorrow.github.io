Broadband Quarter-Wave Impedance Transformer

ECE 6360 Microwave Design  |  Spring 2026  |  RO4003C microstrip  |  Keysight ADS

Overview

Two-part microwave passive design project from ECE 6360. Part 1 designs and optimizes a broadband quarter-wave impedance transformer on microstrip, comparing a single-section baseline against a 3-section Chebyshev transformer. Part 2 (in progress) designs a 3-section branch-line quadrature hybrid coupler targeting 6.75–11.25 GHz. All microstrip lines use RO4003C high-frequency laminate (0.32″ substrate, εˇ = 3.55) with 1 oz (35 µm) copper cladding, designed in Keysight ADS with physical dimensions from LineCalc.

Part 1 — Broadband Quarter-Wave Impedance Transformer

Design Goal

Transform a 50Ω source to a 10Ω load (5:1 impedance ratio) at f₀ = 3 GHz using microstrip quarter-wave sections. Compare a single-section transformer against a 3-section Chebyshev design to quantify the bandwidth improvement.

Section 1: Single-Section Quarter-Wave Transformer

The single-section design uses one λ/4 microstrip section with characteristic impedance:

Z₁ = √(Zˆ · Zᴸ) = √(50 · 10) = 22.36 Ω

Physical dimensions (LineCalc, RO4003C, 3 GHz): W = 218.57 mil, L = 562.50 mil. Simulated bandwidth: 29.667%, consistent with Pozar’s theoretical prediction of 29%.

Section 2: 3-Section Chebyshev Transformer (Optimized)

The 3-section Chebyshev transformer distributes the impedance transformation across three λ/4 sections, with each section impedance set via the geometric-mean staircase:

Z₃ = √(Zˆ · Z₂) = 14.95 Ω   (final step, 50 → Z₂)
Z₂ = √(Zˆ · Zᴸ) = 22.36 Ω   (middle)
Z₁ = √(Z₂ · Zᴸ) = 33.44 Ω   (first step, 50 → Z₂)

After ADS optimizer sweep, the final optimized impedances converged to Z₁ = 33.4 Ω, Z₂ = 21.0 Ω, Z₃ = 13.9 Ω. Optimized bandwidth: 112.333%, nearly a 4× increase over the single-section design.
