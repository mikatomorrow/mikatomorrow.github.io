About

M.S. Electrical Engineering candidate at Georgia Tech (December 2026), B.S. EE with High Honors (May 2025). My work spans RFIC design from the transistor level up — LNAs, mixers, frequency dividers, direct conversion receivers — as well as microwave passive design, phased array and reflectarray antennas, and electronic warfare systems.


I have interned at Leidos (EW / radar systems), Lockheed Martin (Missile Defense Agency), Motorola Solutions (RF design for P25 public-safety radios), and Stryker Robotics. Currently working toward a WiSEE 2026 submission on the PaddleSat passive reflectarray for space solar power.

PaddleSat — Origami Reflectarray for Space Solar Power
Wireless Power Transmission — Spring 2026 — Targeting WiSEE 2026
Passive reflectarray for a solar power satellite using copper-disc elements (phase shift from disc geometry only). No active phase shifters — designed for 20-year space radiation survivability. Compared circular vs. rectangular patch geometries across scan angles; evaluated Gaussian, Dolph–Chebyshev, and Taylor amplitude distributions. Taylor distribution achieved best sidelobe suppression. Fabrication tolerance (0.1 mm) and lattice arrangement analysis.
