// favorite song ever //
MARSHA, THANKK YOU FOR THE DIALECTICS - WILL WOOD
will wood has been a long time favorite since i was in highschool, i went to 2 of his concerts and they have been the best concerts ive been to by far.

this song particularly hits hard, as it focuses on mental health and hits on so many points that i feel strongly about.
  • the perversion of the health, pharmaceutical, & therapeutic industry (“don’t heed no evil wills of moral nihilists”)
  • the social desire to be different and so collecting disorders is approved (“we depress to impress, i guess”)
  • how easy it is to be diagnosed, causing mass misdiagnoses (“If our harmonies don’t sync, we can change our voices, A chorus on condition of our diagnosis”)
all encompassed in an absolutely beautiful instrumental, with lyrics that are poetry on their own. truly an amazing listen.
car seat headrest
csh is my top band right now, especially the older songs. they made their old songs in a car (hence the name) so it all sounds a bit crusty, but in that way it makes it feel real. the voice cracks, the imperfections, the unpolishness screams human and it screams loser, and we're all losers.

top songs: something soon, drunk drivers/killer whales, death at the movies, nervous young inhumans
