Reflectarray with Tapered Hexagonal Grid for Space-Based WPT
ECE Wireless Power Transmission  —  Spring 2026  —  targeting WiSEE 2026  —  w/ A. Abrams, C. Merkt, X. Lin, G. Durgin
Circular-patch reflectarray on hexagonal grid with passive Gaussian aperture taper (σ = 0.70) for space-based WPT at 2.45 GHz. 334° phase range (+52° over square-patch baseline), −24 dB SLL, 99.3% beam efficiency, >40 dB cross-pol isolation. Passive design maintains performance over 20-year GEO mission; delivers 18.5 MWh more cumulative energy than active Chebyshev baseline.
