Reflectarray with Tapered Hexagonal Grid Design for Microwave Power Transmission
ECE Wireless Power Transmission  —  Spring 2026  —  targeting WiSEE 2026
Circular-patch reflectarray on a hexagonal grid with passive Gaussian aperture taper (σ = 0.70) for space-based WPT at 2.45 GHz. 334° phase range (+52° over square-patch baseline), −24 dB SLL, 99.3% beam efficiency, >40 dB cross-pol isolation, zero active electronics. Passive design maintains performance over 20-year GEO mission; delivers 18.5 MWh more cumulative energy than active Chebyshev baseline.

Survey of Benchmarking Methods for Quantum Logic Gates
ECE  —  November 2025  —  w/ Avery Mahan, Eric Yan, Tianyi Yu
